<CaseSection title="The Challenge & Research">
  <CasePoint label="The Brief">
    Antwerp attracts plenty of shoppers and tourists but lacks a strong Gen Z presence. The task was
    to conceptualise and build a digital solution to fix this.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="The Insight">
    Gen Z values the company of friends over the actual destination. But friend group plans
    frequently fail because the designated initiative taker often gets left on read in the group
    chat.
  </CasePoint>
  <CaseImage
    label="Group Chat UI"
    size="narrow"
    tone={3}
    caption="The problem in one screen: a trip proposal left on read in the group chat."
  />
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The Hook & Solution">
  <CasePoint label="The Concept">
    Make Antwerp the city where plans actually make it out of the group chat.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="The Product">
    Plan A is a website where a friend group plans a trip to Antwerp while playing an Antwerp-themed
    game. The player with the highest score wins a free drink from a local bar.
  </CasePoint>
  <CaseImage label="Hero App Mockup" size="narrow" tone={0} caption="Plan A on a smartphone." />
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Discovery & The Initiator's Journey">
  <CasePoint label="Discovery">
    Travel influencers promote the platform through short videos on TikTok, Instagram and other
    social media.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="Taking Action">
    The initiative taker sets an initial game score and fills in the trip details: dates, length of
    stay, budget, preferences and group size.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="Sharing">
    They enter their email, set a time limit for friends to play, and share the link in the group
    chat. If every invited player joins, the planning is sent automatically. Otherwise it is sent
    when the timer ends.
  </CasePoint>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The Game Mechanics">
  <CasePoint label="Gameplay">
    The game works like the Google dinosaur game: players tap to jump over obstacles and can
    practise as often as they like.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="Design">
    The background shows Antwerp eras, diamonds and historic scenes. Extra points become
    collectibles (a smiley, a coupon, a camera, a beer) that point to things to do in Antwerp.
  </CasePoint>
  <CaseImage
    label="Game Interface"
    size="wide"
    ratio="16 / 9"
    tone={2}
    caption="The pixel-style game, with the Antwerp background and collectibles."
  />
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The Friends' Experience & Reward">
  <CasePoint label="Joining In">
    Friends open the link, view the initiator's plan, add their own availability and play the game
    to beat the score.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="Choosing the Winner">
    The final trip date is the one most players chose. The highest score wins a coupon for a free
    drink, as long as they picked the final date. If not, the coupon goes to the highest-scoring
    player who did.
  </CasePoint>
  <CasePoint label="The Coupon">
    A QR code links to a random bar based on the selected preference tags, drawn from a database of
    three places per tag.
  </CasePoint>
  <CaseImage
    label="QR Code Flow"
    size="wide"
    tone={1}
    caption="Voting results and the winning coupon."
  />
</CaseSection>
