## Overview

Antwerp draws plenty of shoppers and tourists but few Gen Z visitors. The brief was to conceptualise and build a digital solution that changes that. The result is Plan A: a website where a friend group plans a trip to Antwerp while playing an Antwerp-themed game, and the highest score wins a free drink at a local bar.

The project brought UX, design and development together, and I worked on it as UX designer and digital designer.

### How Might We

How might we get a friend group's trip idea out of the group chat and into a real plan, without relying on one person to chase everyone?

## The Challenge & Research

### The Brief

Antwerp attracts plenty of shoppers and tourists but lacks a strong Gen Z presence. The task was to conceptualise and build a digital solution to fix this.

### The Insight

Gen Z values the company of friends over the actual destination. But friend group plans frequently fail because the designated initiative taker often gets left on read in the group chat.

## The Hook & Solution

### The Concept

Make Antwerp the city where plans actually make it out of the group chat.

### The Product

Plan A is a website where a friend group plans a trip to Antwerp while playing an Antwerp-themed game. The player with the highest score wins a free drink from a local bar.

## The Game Mechanics

### Gameplay

The game works like the Google dinosaur game: players tap to jump over obstacles and can practise as often as they like.

### Design

The background shows Antwerp eras, diamonds and historic scenes. Extra points become collectibles (a smiley, a coupon, a camera, a beer) that point to things to do in Antwerp.

## The Friends' Experience & Reward

### Joining In

Friends open the link, view the initiator's plan, add their own availability and play the game to beat the score.

### Choosing the Winner

The final trip date is the one most players chose. The highest score wins a coupon for a free drink, as long as they picked the final date. If not, the coupon goes to the highest-scoring player who did.

### The Coupon

A QR code links to a random bar based on the selected preference tags, drawn from a database of three places per tag.
