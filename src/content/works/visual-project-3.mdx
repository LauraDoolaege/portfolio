<h2 class="tag">The brief</h2>

**Disprove a common misconception in a creative way.** We took on the myth that chocolate causes acne.

<h2 class="tag">The idea</h2>

Miss Chocolate is an **interactive digital court case** with one goal: to **acquit her of causing acne in the first degree.** The story is told as a **case file**, from her history to the proof to the verdict, using illustration and digital storytelling to **turn a skincare myth into a trial.**
