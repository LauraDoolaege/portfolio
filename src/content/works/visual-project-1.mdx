<h2 class="tag">The brief</h2>

Thrive, **Belgium's first sports beer**, needed a **concept for a campaign website** to promote one of their other beers, Thrive Unwind or Thrive Play, **in their own unique way.** This one is for Thrive Unwind, an alcohol-free, magnesium sports beer, taken beyond pure athletics to speak to everyone, not only the people who train.

<h2 class="tag">The idea</h2>

Working Nine to Thrive treats **everyday life as the workout.** Photo-bashed puns follow **Tom, a man with a beer can for a head,** as he recovers from a 500m dash to catch the train, crunching numbers and pitching ideas. Life takes a toll on the body, so **Thrive Unwind is for everyone.**
