<Phase num="01" title="The Problem" />

People already know what they should do. The gap is between knowing and doing, and the product has to meet them in that gap.

Our starting question was simple: what small push makes the right action the easy one?

<Phase num="02" title="My Role & Process" />

I worked as strategist and wireframer. That meant turning what we learned about real behavior into interactions, then sketching and wireframing them in Figma so the team could react to something concrete.

<PullQuote source="The rule we designed by">
  If the value proposition isn't clear within 3 seconds, UI polish won't save the experience.
</PullQuote>

Huyen and I worked side by side, trading wireframes and critiques until each nudge earned its place on the screen.

<Phase num="03" title="The Solution" />

Clear, nudge-based interactions that tie what users want to what the product needs them to do, with the value visible straight away.
