## Overview

Nexxus is a group assignment about safety on a generational spaceship: how do you keep a closed community secure for a very long time without making people feel monitored and controlled? I worked on it as UX researcher and UX/UI designer.

### How Might We

How might we maintain social safety for a long period of time in a closed environment, without creating a feeling of excessive monitoring or control?

### The Insight

Social safety works better than systemic monitoring. Think of the neighbourhood grandma who keeps an eye on the street: people look out for each other, and nobody feels watched.

### Validation

We tested the concept with users to check that it was clear, relevant and desirable.

## The Solution

### Home Page & Dashboard

The central hub. Users open a personal dashboard with their tracked data, such as heartbeat or body temperature, and can manage subscriptions to unlock more detailed, continuously monitored data.

### Reward System

Users earn optional Helper Points by assisting other astronauts, good karma made visible. The points can be exchanged for rewards such as a free croissant, a cocktail, a massage or a dinner.
